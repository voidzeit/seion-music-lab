# SEION Project 001 — Sound and release gates

## Evidence levels

- **Observed**: inspected audio result and playback notes by a listener.
- **Derived**: measurable spectrum, tempo, chroma, onsets and level estimates.
- **Hypothesized**: artist-character descriptors not measured on audio.
- **Proposed**: new generated MIDI or selected factory preset, not yet heard.

The harmonic correlation 0.647 is not 64.7% confidence. The tonal centre
must be rechecked against the original recording and the selected remix hook.

## Musical acceptance

1. Three 16-bar A/B/C MIDI versions are structurally valid at 158 BPM.
2. Distinct rhythmic bass architectures and suitable kick/bass sound design.
3. Three real WAV previews and separate stems, no unintended silence or clipping.
4. Human review on groove, bass/kick interaction, melodic identity, dynamics,
   contrast, recognizable but transformed musical logic and originality.
5. One 16-bar experiment approved before expansion to a 32-bar drop.
6. No 160-bar arrangement generated from an unheard placeholder.
7. No final audio quality or genre-originality scores without evidence.

## Technical acceptance

- Ardour headless must not overwrite target sessions or prior renders.
- Every export reports actual success; no quiet acceptance of failed WAVs.
- Plugin kits must be resolvable on the target computer.
- CI unit tests and ruff passing; Windows/Surge integration separately verified.
- Keep original commercial recording, stems and extracted phrases local only.
- Document permission from composition/master owners before commercial release.
